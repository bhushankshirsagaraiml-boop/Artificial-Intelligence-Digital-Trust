About Artificial Intelligence

Understanding the core concepts that power today's intelligent systems.

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks which normally require human intelligence — such as reasoning, learning, perception, and decision-making. Rather than following rigid, hand-coded rules, modern AI systems learn patterns from data and improve their performance over time.

AI today powers everything from voice assistants and recommendation engines to medical diagnosis tools and autonomous vehicles — making it one of the most transformative technologies of this century.

Categories

Types of AI

Narrow AI

Designed for a single, specific task — like spam filtering or facial recognition. This is the most common form of AI in use today.

General AI

A theoretical form of AI with human-level cognitive ability across any intellectual task — still an active area of research.

Super AI

A hypothetical future stage where AI surpasses human intelligence across every domain — a topic of ongoing ethical debate.

Reactive & Limited Memory AI

Systems classified by capability: reactive machines respond to inputs directly, while limited-memory AI learns from recent historical data.

Core Pillars

Machine Learning, Deep Learning & Generative AI

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI where systems learn patterns directly from data instead of being explicitly programmed. It includes supervised learning (learning from labeled examples), unsupervised learning (finding hidden patterns), and reinforcement learning (learning through trial, error and reward).

Deep Learning is a specialized subset of ML that uses multi-layered artificial neural networks to automatically extract complex patterns from large volumes of data. It powers technologies like image recognition, speech-to-text, and natural language understanding.

Generative AI refers to models capable of creating new content — text, images, audio, code, or video — based on patterns learned from massive training datasets. It represents one of the fastest-growing and most publicly visible areas of AI innovation.
