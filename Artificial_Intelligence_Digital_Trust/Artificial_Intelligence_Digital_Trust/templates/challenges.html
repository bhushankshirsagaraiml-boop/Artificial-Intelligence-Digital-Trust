AI Challenges

Understanding the risks that must be managed for AI to remain trustworthy.

Deepfakes

AI-generated synthetic media that convincingly mimics real people's voices and faces, posing serious risks for misinformation and fraud.

Bias in AI

Models trained on skewed or unrepresentative data can produce unfair or discriminatory outcomes against certain groups.

Privacy Issues

Large-scale data collection required to train AI models raises serious concerns about consent and personal privacy.

Misinformation

Generative AI can be used to rapidly produce and spread false or misleading information at scale.

Security Risks

AI systems themselves can be targeted through adversarial attacks, data poisoning, or model theft.

Job Automation

Automation of routine cognitive and physical tasks raises concerns about workforce displacement.

Data Leakage

Sensitive data used in training or inference can unintentionally be exposed or memorized by AI models.
