As Artificial Intelligence becomes deeply embedded in daily life — from recommendation systems to autonomous decision-making — the question of trust becomes central. This project examines both sides of that coin: the incredible capability of AI, and the responsibility required to deploy it safely, transparently and ethically.

Did You Know?

AI Facts

The term "Artificial Intelligence" was first coined in 1956 at the Dartmouth Conference.

Explainable AI (XAI) helps convert "black box" models into decisions humans can actually understand.

Federated Learning allows AI models to learn from data without that data ever leaving the user's device.

Many countries now require "algorithmic transparency" reports for high-risk AI systems.
